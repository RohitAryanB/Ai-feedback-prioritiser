# CivicPulse — AI Community Intelligence

---

### **Overview**
**CivicPulse** is an AI-powered community intelligence platform designed to bridge the gap between citizen feedback and government action. By aggregating data from public complaints, suggestions, and surveys, the system utilizes **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** to automatically categorize issues, assess their severity, and route them to the appropriate authorities in real-time.

Instead of relying on manual surveys, CivicPulse turns raw public sentiment into actionable data, helping local leaders and government agencies prioritize resources where they are needed most.

---

### **Core Innovation**
* **Automated Problem Detection**: Replaces manual data collection with an AI-driven intake system that processes information instantly.
* **Intelligent Prioritization**: Uses AI to rank issues based on severity, frequency, and community impact.
* **Hyper-Local Insights**: Provides data specific to neighborhoods or wards rather than generalized reports.
* **Multi-Sector Utility**: Designed for a seamless experience across government bodies, schools, and non-profits.

---

### **Key Features**
* **AI-Driven Intake Form**: A smart submission portal that handles complaints, suggestions, and general feedback.
* **Real-Time Processing**: An integrated "AI Processing" engine that performs NLP analysis, keyword extraction, and department routing.
* **Dynamic Dashboard**: A comprehensive visual interface featuring:
    * **Live Statistics**: Tracking total, pending, and critical submissions.
    * **Geospatial Insights**: Identifying top reported areas and problem zones.
    * **Category Breakdowns**: Visualizing issue distribution (Infrastructure, Environment, Health, etc.).
* **Issue Tracking System**: A dedicated tracking portal where citizens can enter ticket IDs to view AI analysis outcomes and resolution timelines.

---

### **Technical Stack**
* **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3 (using Syne and JetBrains Mono typography), and Vanilla JavaScript.
* **Styling**: Custom CSS variables for a high-contrast, dark-mode civic interface.
* **Intelligence**: Integration with Large Language Models (LLMs) for high-fidelity civic analysis and sentiment categorization.
* **Storage**: Persistent local storage to maintain complaint records across sessions.
